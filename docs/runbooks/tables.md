# Table operations

One table package includes the declared guest admissions, inclusions and minimum-spend credit. `fullPriceMinor` is the entire contracted price, not an extra reservation fee. Minimum spend is included in this price; purchases beyond it are the venue's separate sale. The balance is due before admission. The seller must publish accurate inclusions, tax input and terms before taking payment.

1. A venue owner/manager/editor with `venue.edit` creates a layout at `POST /v1/seller/venues/:id/layouts`. Provide unique zones and stable physical table IDs, normalized center/size/rotation, seated capacity, a reason and the latest version (zero for the first). Reuse physical IDs when moving furniture on a future layout. Layout history is immutable.
2. A host publisher pins a layout with `POST /v1/seller/occurrences/:id/layout`. Once offers exist the pin is fixed. Create immutable packages at `POST /v1/seller/occurrences/:id/table-offers`; use timezone-correct slot offsets inside the occurrence. Withdraw a package through `/v1/seller/table-offers/:id/publication`, then create a replacement for a new price or terms. Withdrawal preserves existing holds and purchases.
3. Customers obtain `/v1/occurrences/:id/tables` and submit `/v1/table-reservations` with offer version, party size, full/deposit mode and an idempotency key. Availability is advisory; reservation acquires event and physical-table locks. PostgreSQL independently rejects overlapping held/confirmed ranges, including across occurrences and staff blocks. Each guest uses an admission from total occurrence capacity. Holds expire within ten minutes or at slot start, whichever comes first; unresolved payments keep allocations until reconciled.
4. Use the returned order with the shared terms/payment/recovery APIs. Full-package commission is collected in the online payment; a deposit below this fee is rejected. Fulfillment creates one QR per guest. The native/web booking screens display the package, paid amounts, balance, table reference and full-party cancellation.
5. Door staff scan a ticket or enter the reservation reference. `/v1/seller/table-reservations/:id` returns scoped balance and receipt records. Record actual received money with `/collections` (`cash` or `external-card`, exact minor amount, receipt reference, reason, idempotency key). A venue/method/reference is unique across all reservations. No external terminal card data enters Clubby. Unpaid balances prevent admission; table entry opens thirty minutes before its slot and closes at slot end. Managers may override the time window with an audited admission reason, but cannot bypass a balance or inactive right.
6. A manager records the full reversal of a receipt at `/v1/seller/door-collections/:id/reverse` with a distinct reference/reason. A mistaken amount is corrected by reversing it and recording the correct amount. Financial records are append-only. Collection reports do not represent Stripe transfers or fiscal invoices.
7. Customer refunds select the complete party through the shared order refund command. Online payment and eligible commission are refunded through Stripe; any door money remains a visible `refund-review` obligation until the venue records its return. Layout changes preserve snapshots. Event cancellation **or rescheduling** immediately disables table entry and table offers and starts durable cancellation/refund work; appointments are never silently shifted to an overlapping time.
8. Mark a paid reservation `/no-show` only after its purchased grace period and before any guest is admitted. The sole supported policy explicitly retains paid amounts and waives uncollected balances. Correcting a retained door receipt later does not recreate waived debt. Admission progress is derived from individual guest scans; it is not simulated occupancy.
9. Block a physical table at `/v1/seller/tables/:id/blocks` with a timezone-correct interval, reason and idempotency key. The same exclusion constraint protects blocks, holds and bookings. List active venue blocks and release them through `/v1/seller/table-blocks/:id/release`. Blocks cannot displace a customer's live allocation.

Troubleshooting: use order/refund/provider reconciliation for uncertain money; never delete allocations to resolve a payment delay. An unresolved refund/dispute preserves its explicit state. Review door obligations and receipts separately from online provider balances. Exported privacy records include the customer's reservation and relevant door receipts. No business account, tax approval, real terminal integration or Stripe-hosted payment was fabricated for local testing.
