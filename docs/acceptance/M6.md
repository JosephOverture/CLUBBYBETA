# M6 — group funding and collaboration review

Local engineering gate, 2026-09-23. M5's evidence gate passed before this work. Migration `006_groups_collaboration.sql` is applied to persistent local development data and is immutable.

Implemented W15/W16 and their finance, notification, privacy and client integrations. A group reserves one existing exclusive table allocation, with a bounded deadline, guest limit, immutable funding contract and explicitly responsible owner. Email-bound invitations are single-use, expiring and revocable. Ownership requires acceptance of the remaining balance. Members cannot be removed to take a funded person's place; departures preserve their own payment and admission records.

Unequal contributions each have an immutable order, one real provider attempt and an exact fee/tax share. Unresolved payments reserve the target amount, preventing overfunding races. Only authoritative payments fund the group. The parent reservation cannot be separately charged and has no duplicate sale journal or duplicate gross sales. Confirmation allocates member tickets and preserves the usual table balance. Deadlines honor funding recorded before the deadline even if a worker executes later. Partial funding expires through cancellation/refund before allocation release; unknown provider outcomes hold stock. Late successes, contributor withdrawals, refunds and recovered money use the original payer's payment.

Financial-risk changes block rights in the same transaction rather than waiting for polling or a worker. Partial external refunds and chargeback losses are subtracted before subsequent refunds. Chargeback losses/recoveries have separate postings. Recovered captures for cancelled groups are returned to their original payers. A shortfall after admission or a no-show enters a scoped seller review rather than automatically refunding others' consumed service. Authorized decisions and door-money obligations remain explicit and audited.

Plain-text chat is ordered, paginated, bounded and idempotent. Authenticated five-second cursor polling rechecks membership and resumes after foreground/reconnection. Blocking filters chat while preserving essential financial events. Reports and reasoned support/admin moderation are implemented with shared-client tools. Messages never affect funding. Retention removes message bodies in bounded batches ninety days after the occurrence; privacy exports include authored content, reports, blocks, accepted terms and financial records. Worker scheduling uses last-check ordering so old review cases do not indefinitely monopolize deadline processing.

Both clients include group creation and terms, invitation review/join/revocation, unequal contribution checkout through the existing native/web payment UI, payment/refund records, member tickets, balances, ownership/leave actions, chat/reconnect, blocking/reporting, moderation and seller financial-review controls. External identity/payment secrets never appear in group projections or update payloads.

Review corrections covered late-worker deadline handling, contributor tax rounding, duplicate parent revenue, partial external refunds, partial lost/recovered chargebacks, consumed-service review, owner-removal safeguards and fairness of maintenance. The detailed rules and operational routes are in `docs/architecture/group-funding.md` and `docs/runbooks/groups.md`.

Evidence: `pnpm check` passes both typechecks, **104 tests across nine files**, and backend/worker builds. New real-PostgreSQL tests cover verified invite/revoke/expiry/member capacity, web/native unequal payments, competing final contributions, exact fee/tax totals, shared allocation and member tickets, full cancellation to original payers, provider outage/late success, withdrawal/replacement funding, external partial refunds, partial chargeback loss/recovery, ownership responsibility, typed authenticated HTTP contracts, deadline worker lag, empty expiry, chat idempotency/resume/revocation/rate limiting, blocking, moderation and consumed-service financial decisions. Both web and iOS/Hermes exports pass, production dependency audit has zero advisories, and persistent PostgreSQL/API/worker startup passes.

Conclusion: M6 is locally complete and M7 may finish the remaining product and operational capabilities. This gate does not claim live Stripe, email-domain, signed-device, production-hosting or App Store certification; those require the accounts/device evidence explicitly absent under the user's local preparation scope.
